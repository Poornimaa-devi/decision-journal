# Day 43 — Authentication Concepts

## What I worked on
Studied authentication theory — no coding today. Focused on understanding 
the difference between authentication and authorization, and why JWT is 
the right approach for this project.

## What I learned
- HTTP's stateless nature and why that requires a separate mechanism (sessions 
  or tokens) to "remember" a logged-in user
- Session-based vs token-based authentication, and why JWT fits a REST API 
  with a future React frontend better
