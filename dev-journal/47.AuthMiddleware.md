# Day 47 — Authentication Middleware

## What I worked on
Built middleware/authMiddleware.js to verify JWTs on incoming requests and 
attach the decoded user info to req.user.

## What I learned
- jwt.verify() is synchronous, unlike bcrypt/Mongoose — needs try/catch, 
  not .then()/.catch()
- Why login/signup routes must stay unprotected, while everything else can 
  require a valid token
- The Authorization: Bearer <token> header convention
