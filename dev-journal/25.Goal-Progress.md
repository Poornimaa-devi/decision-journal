# Day 25 Development Journal

## Today's Goal

Add progress tracking to every goal and automatically determine whether a goal is completed.

---

## What I Built

- Added a Progress input to the Goal Form.
- Stored progress in every goal object.
- Displayed progress on every Goal Card.
- Added a progress bar.
- Automatically calculated completed status.

---

## Biggest Learning

I learned about derived data and how applications can calculate values instead of storing duplicate information.

---

## Hardest Concept

Understanding that the completed status should depend on the progress value rather than being entered separately.

---

## How Progress Tracking Improved My Project

My Decision Journal now provides more detailed information about every goal. Instead of only knowing whether a goal is completed, users can track how much progress they have made.
