# Day 25 - Goal Progress

## What is progress tracking?

Progress tracking measures how much of a goal has been completed. Instead of simply marking a goal as completed or not completed, it shows the percentage of work finished.

---

## Why is progress better than only "Completed"?

A completed status only tells whether the goal is finished. Progress tracking gives more detailed information by showing how far the user has reached, such as 25%, 50%, or 80%.

---

## What is derived data?

Derived data is information calculated from existing data instead of being stored separately. In my project, the completed status is derived from the progress value.

---

## Why calculate status from progress?

Calculating the status from progress avoids inconsistent data. If the progress reaches 100%, the application automatically marks the goal as completed.

---

## How will progress improve my Decision Journal?

Progress tracking helps users monitor their goals more effectively. It provides a clear view of how close they are to completing each goal and encourages continuous improvement.
